Explain how microscopy autofocus works.
✳ ASSISTANT

From a blurred image to a focus estimate

Autofocus estimates the distance between the current imaging plane and the best-focus plane.

A signed defocus model predicts both the direction and the distance of the required movement.

def focus_step(image):
    dz = model.predict(image)
    stage.move_z(-dz)

Use a small correction step near focus, then verify the result with a new image.
